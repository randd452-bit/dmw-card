THE SETTING · QUAKE-SHATTERED TOKYO

"Candy buys three days. Three days buys one more match."

A prison behind a cheerful facade — Deadman Wonderland, where inmates perform as theme park attractions for tourists by day and vanish into the system by night. Lethal collars inject poison. Candy antidotes keep the clock ticking. Cast Points reduce survival to economics. Every breath costs something, every meal costs more.

Below the surface, in the hidden G Ward, Deadmen — humans infected with a parasite that lets them weaponize their own blood — fight in secret death matches called Carnival Corpse. The audience pays. The slot machine takes. The losers leave pieces of themselves behind on the surgical table. No one outside knows. The mascots keep smiling.

◆ THE ARCS ◆

From Arrival to Final

Six arcs. Thirty-plus characters. One collapsing system.
